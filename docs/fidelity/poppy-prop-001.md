# poppy-prop-001 (image-to-3D) — VERDICT: SHIP (first verdict was wrong)

Tripo image-to-3D from the bloom crop, 9cr. The first version of this log
called an "invented terracotta planter" -- there is no planter. The generation
is a RELIEF of the bloom, his impasto given genuine depth: lumpy wet paint,
smeared crimsons, yellow flecks, the dark seed centre. What read as a pot was
the relief's beige BACKING viewed from behind, because the placement had its
painted face turned away from the arrival.

Two lessons for the court itself:
1. Judge a 3D asset from a standalone render before judging it in the scene --
   the scene adds placement errors that masquerade as generation errors.
2. A relief has a front. Orientation is part of placement, not an afterthought.

The pagoda prop (pagoda-prop-001, same run): full 3D, grey tiers, warm lit
windows, crimson roof -- SHIP without caveats. 54MB -> 1.7MB and 56MB -> 0.9MB
through draco+webp at 1024.
