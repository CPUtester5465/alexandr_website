# Chemistry — «Химия»

Dimension lore. Slug `chemistry`. A subject world: no painting to sample, so
the palette is the study's materials plus one rationed colour — `#EDE6D2`
paper · `#C8B392` plaster · `#8A8578` stone · `#4A4741` graphite · `#23241F`
ink · `#3E7C74` reagent green. Terrain FACETED (ridged, terrace 2), structures
CRYSTALS, sky paper. Green marks **only** the chemically true — the way red
marks the measured in mathematics. No chemical fact below is invented.

## 1. The record

Prize-winner, «Эстафета знаний – 2026» intellectual tournament, natural
sciences, chemistry — Призёр, «Эстафета знаний – 2026»,
естественно-научное направление, химия. Nothing else is claimed.

## 2. The world's story

This world is a supersaturated solution settling into order. Once everything
here was dissolved — stone, salt, quartz, all in one still liquid — then the
long evaporation began. The ground is what has already come out of solution:
ridged and faceted, every facet a face that grew rather than a rock that
broke. The air carries the rest: ink-fleck particles drift like dust in a
sunbeam, and where one meets a crystal face it slows, queues, and settles —
so the spires stand taller than the last visitor found them. Nothing erupts,
nothing bubbles: the drama is the quietest chemistry has — matter choosing a
shape and holding to it. The world is never finished crystallising, on
purpose; finished, it would be a mineral cabinet, not an experiment.

## 3. Signature structures

**Halite courts — Дворы галита.** Clusters of perfect cubes, 1–4 blocks a
side, plaster faces with stone-shaded edges, half-sunk in the ground; a seeded
few grow as stepped hopper cubes — square terraces recessing inward. The
chemistry: table salt (NaCl, галит) is cubic — every grain is a tiny cube, and
hoppers are salt grown too fast. Lore: order nobody arranged.

**Quartz spires — Кварцевые шпили.** The main crystal pass, disciplined:
six-sided prisms (voxel hexagon in cross-section) rising 4–15 blocks from
ink-block roots to a stepped point; plaster bodies, graphite shadow faces, one
green block at the tip of a seeded few — the ones that grew true. The
chemistry: quartz (SiO₂) grows six-sided prisms ending in a point (trigonal,
hexagonal family); snowflakes have six corners for the same reason — the
lattice decides the shape. Lore: nobody built these; they grew.

**Fluorite octahedra — Октаэдры флюорита.** Voxel octahedra — two stepped
pyramids base to base, 3–7 blocks tall — on graphite plinths; the only
structures allowed to be mostly reagent green, because fluorite genuinely is.
The chemistry: fluorite (CaF₂) is cubic yet splits cleanly along octahedral
planes, and its green is real. Lore: green where nature spent it first.

**The periodic terrace — Периодическая терраса.** One landform per region:
the terrain's terracing gathered into architecture. Each terrace is a period;
stone pedestals run downslope in columns as groups; rows lengthen going down —
2, then 8, then 8, then 18 — and one seeded column is seamed green top to
bottom. The chemistry: the table's staircase shape is structure, not
decoration — periods lengthen because atoms gain shells, and a column is a
family that behaves alike. Lore: the world's order, written down not grown.

## 4. Ambient life

**The solute — Взвесь.** Instanced ink flecks in slow Brownian drift. Near
any crystal face they slow, queue, and settle one at a time onto the lattice —
growth you can stand and watch. The chemistry: dissolving and crystallising
are the same particles changing direction, and the jitter is real (Brownian).

**Pool breath — Дыхание заводи.** Over still pools, plaster flecks rise and
vanish at a fixed height; under cold graphite overhangs nearby they reappear
and drop. Evaporation and condensation at arm's length. No steam — breath.

## 5. Activities

1. **Grow a crystal — Вырасти кристалл.** A plot near the door: an alum seed
   on an ink thread over a basin. Its size derives from real days since your
   first visit (seeded, time-bucketed) — return next week and it is genuinely
   larger, octahedral at every stage. The chemistry: slow growth from
   saturated solution gives large clean crystals. A plaque owns the famous
   gap: the school crystal everyone grows is copper sulphate, a deep blue the
   palette cannot say — so this world grows honestly colourless alum (квасцы).
2. **Match the habit — Подбери форму.** Loose carryable crystals — cube,
   hopper cube, six-sided prism, octahedron — near four labelled plinths. A
   true match seams green; a false one stays graphite. Geometry is the key.
3. **Walk the table.** Stand on any pedestal of the periodic terrace and its
   whole column lights green — same group, same family, a checkable fact.
   Stand on a row and nothing lights: a period is a journey, not a family.
4. **The saturation pool — Насыщенный раствор.** Push plaster salt blocks
   into a marked pool; each dissolves into drifting solute — until the pool
   holds no more and the next block sits whole on the bottom. Return later:
   the water has fallen and a fringe of true cubes rims the edge. Solubility
   has a limit; evaporation gives back exactly what dissolved.
5. **The copper lantern — Медный фонарь.** One lantern, one blocky green
   flame. The plaque: copper salts colour a flame green (sodium yellow,
   lithium crimson) — green being the only flame this palette can tell the
   truth in, the world keeps just the one.

## 7. What would ruin it

- **Bubbling green goo.** The big one. No vats, no slime, no toxic barrels.
  Green here is reagent green — the colour of the checked-true and of minerals
  that really are green. Goo spends the world's one colour on a lie.
- **Mad-lab glamour and fantasy alchemy.** No explosions as reward, no smoking
  flasks, no transmutation circles, no potions with powers. The register is a
  lab notebook: patient, dated, checked — a crystal that grew true, not a
  reaction that got away.
- **Gem-shop crystals.** No rainbow sparkle, no humming "crystal energy", no
  generic diamond prop for every mineral. Each crystal wears its own system's
  real geometry — a cube where an octahedron belongs is the fabricated-award
  failure in mineral form.
