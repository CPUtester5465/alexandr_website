# Cup & Apple, Quiet Light — Чашка и яблоко, тихий свет

Dimension lore for slug `cup-apple`. One of the two graphite worlds — "the calm".
Palette is the sampled sheet, nothing else: `#A2A6A6` (surface), `#B1B7B9` (pale),
`#8A8E92` (deep), `#9DA5AF` (accent), `#6D6F6F` (stem/shadow), `#86877F` (core).
Taste bar: Morandi. The bravest world is the quietest.

## 1. The painting, read closely

An enamel mug and an apple lying on its side, on a table, in light that comes
from nowhere in particular — upper left, diffuse, patient. The mug's rim is a
worked ellipse: you can see it was tried more than once and settled. Its handle
holds a pocket of untouched paper — the brightest thing on the sheet is where
the pencil never went. The apple lies tipped toward us, blossom end out, and its
dark centre dot is the single deepest mark in the drawing. The background is
long diagonal hatching; the table is horizontal hatching; the two objects sit
where the two directions meet. Shadows pool to the right and under — the light
is agreed on and kept, edge to edge. Nothing is dramatic. Everything is decided.

## 2. The world's story

This is a world made of pencil, and it obeys drawing logic, not painting logic.
Paint covers; pencil accumulates and can be taken away again — so here, light
is not something added but something *removed*, and the brightest ground is
where the world has been erased back to paper. The construction was never fully
cleaned up: guide ellipses, axis lines, the light scaffolding a careful hand
draws before committing — all still faintly there, because in a drawing the
thinking is allowed to show. Nothing was ever wet here; nothing dripped or
rushed. The terrain is table-flat (amplitude 0.9 — the flattest in all fourteen
worlds), there is no wind, the fog is the tone of a soft-pencil mid-grey, and
the horizon is hatching. This is the world you go to when the others are loud.
It does not reward you for hurrying, because there is nothing here that hurries.

## 3. Signature structures

No structures generate yet (`structure: NOTHING` in specs). These are the
recurring types the world wants, in its grammar — all grey, all seeded.

**The Mug — Кружка.** One per region, landmark scale: a hollow block cylinder
~14 blocks tall, ~10 wide, body in pale `#B1B7B9` with the shadow side stepped
down through `#A2A6A6` to `#8A8E92`. The handle is a squared arch, 3 blocks
thick, walkable — and its inside face is the world's lightest tone, the
untouched-paper pocket from the sheet. Climbable via the handle; the rim is a
ring path; the inside is empty, quiet, and slightly darker. Lore: the well of
quiet. Sitting on the rim is the world's summit experience.

**The Apple — Яблоко.** A lying block sphere ~8 blocks across in `#9DA5AF` and
`#A2A6A6`, tipped so its blossom-end dimple faces sideways, with a single
`#6D6F6F` core block at the centre of the dimple — the darkest mark in the
world, as on the sheet. Each apple pools a flat one-block shadow skirt
(`#6D6F6F`) on its light-agreed side. Lore: the weight. You circle it to see
the tone turn.

**Construction lines — Линии построения.** Thin raised rails, 1 block wide,
half a block tall, in pale `#B1B7B9`: great ellipses ringing each mug and
apple at ground level, and straight axis lines running between them. Aligned,
not jittered — worked-out things sit where they were put. Some fade to broken
dashes: half-erased. Lore: the thinking left visible. Walking a line end to
end is how you learn where the objects were going to be before they were.

**Eraser glades — Поляны ластика.** Irregular ground patches, 4–9 blocks
across, where the surface lifts one tone to the palest grey — hatching taken
away, paper showing. They are the brightest places in the world and the only
"weather" it has. Lore: light, here, is something that was removed.

## 4. Ambient life

What moves in a still life is the light, and almost nothing else. Graphite dust
motes drift down through the fog like the finest snow, settling toward the
eraser glades. Shadow skirts creep — over long minutes, seeded, imperceptibly —
as the diffuse light leans. Far off, once in a long while, a single band of
hatching on the horizon re-strokes itself, one tick of movement at the edge of
vision, and is still again. No birds, no wind, no faces. Deliberate scarcity:
each moving thing exists so the stillness has something to be measured against.

## 5. Activities

Quiet verbs only; the lore is the substrate, not a quest log.

- **Finding the light — Найти свет.** Cross the fog to the brightest eraser
  glade in the region and stand in it. That's the whole activity. That's enough.
- **Tracing the ellipse — Обвести эллипс.** Walk a construction ellipse all
  the way around a mug without stepping off. Where you've walked, the line
  settles one tone darker — a committed stroke over a guide.
- **Arranging — Расставить.** Loose small blocks (pebble scale, all six greys)
  can be picked up and set down. The world asks only that you put things where
  they look right. Your still life stays, in your seed.
- **Sitting in the shadow — Посидеть в тени.** The cool side of the apple.
  The dust is easiest to see from inside a shadow, looking out at the light.
- **Counting the strokes — Считать штрихи.** Follow one band of horizontal
  hatching from where you stand until it dissolves into the fog.

## 7. Three kitsch traps

1. **Smuggling colour.** No "one red apple" as a reward, no warm sepia grade,
   no golden hour. The sampling returned six greys because he drew in graphite.
   Grey is not a placeholder waiting for colour; it is the palette (Law 2).
2. **Making quiet cute.** No sleeping mug with a face, no snoring, no lullaby
   plinks, no cozy-core steam curling from the cup. Morandi, not a nursery.
   The dignity of the objects is the point; charm would spend it.
3. **Performing the drawing.** No giant ghost pencil sketching the world in
   front of you, no time-lapse of the scene "being drawn". The drawing is
   finished. Construction lines are residue of thought, not a live show —
   the world's modesty about its own making is what makes it believable.
