# When Gravity Sleeps — Когда гравитация спит

Dimension lore for slug `gravity`. Source: `art-originals/When-Gravity-Sleeps.png`.
Terrain RIDGE, structures PILLARS, gravity 20 (of a normal 54), fog 45–300, per
`src/world/dimensions/specs.ts`. Sampled ink greys, the whole law: surface `#A4A3A2`,
deep/core `#575453`, pale `#CBCBCA`, stem `#7A7776`, accent `#918F8E`, accentLit and
sky `#B7B6B5` — paper-grey, not black.

## 1. The painting, read closely

Pen on paper, and the page is full. The cast, counted: a floating island with a
hatched root-beard underneath and a spit trailing right; a mound whose **stippled
cone summit carries an umbrella on a black mast**, two more umbrellas on the
slope, one bent; a **bold solid-black anchor** planted beside them, rope curling;
five **striped trumpet sprouts** — ringed horn-tubes curving from the grass, one
fallen; two upright **striped caterpillar creatures** with whiskered faces, one
grinning. On the right a **ladder of about eleven cats** stacked head on head in
striped scarves, stick arms out, a fanged one on top, two queueing at the base —
reaching for a **crescent moon covered in spiral doodles**, along which a dozen
round-eared **moon spirits** march off into the sky, a lion-maned one seated on
the lower horn. At left, a **bare tree full of owl-leaves** — round-eyed,
open-beaked, more adrift — and **comet-creatures**, open-mouthed bird-fish,
flying above. Elsewhere in the sky: a comet with a concentric snail-shell tail;
a **gridded ball in a black net** (top-left); five spiral galaxies; a ringed
Saturn; a dotted biscuit-moon; a **stitched zigzag seam** down the right edge;
and everywhere the diagonal hatching — a star-wind combing the cosmos one way.

## 2. The world's story

Gravity is asleep here, not gone — and like any sleeper it stirs. Its sleep is
visible: the hatching is its breathing combed across the sky, and now and then a
**hatch-squall** — a band where the strokes crowd dense and dark — drifts over
the island, and inside that band gravity half-wakes: things settle, jumps go
short, the owl-leaves grip their branches — then it passes and all is light
again. The island manages. It pins itself with anchors at the points it cannot
afford to lose, grows trumpets to breathe out what the squalls press in, raises
umbrellas against the stroke-weather, and keeps one patient cat-ladder up for
the spirits' procession — deliberately one cat short.

## 3. Signature structures

**Anchor Post — Якорь.** The darkest object in the world: a shaft of core
`#575453` blocks 4–6 tall, curved block flukes, a one-block ring on top, a rope
of deep blocks curling away, pointing (seeded) to the next anchor. Within six
blocks of one gravity is *awake* — full weight, ordinary jumps. Lore: the
island's pinned points; it holds down what it loves. One per region.

**Trumpet Sprout — Рожок.** A curved column of alternating stem and pale block
rings, 5–10 tall, leaning with the star-wind, flaring to a 3-wide mouth; a seeded
quarter lie fallen. A slow column of pale motes rises from each standing mouth.
Lore: the island breathes through them; step into the exhale and it lifts you.

**Umbrella Mast — Зонт на мачте.** A stem-block mast 5–9 tall on a stippled cone
of accent-flecked surface blocks, topped by a 5×5 pale canopy with an accent rim,
tilted downwind; instanced slabs, nothing moves but the lean. Lore: shelter —
under a canopy a squall's wakefulness does not reach you.

**Cat-Ladder — Кошачья лестница.** A tower of 2×2×2 pale heads with accent
ear-blocks and stem-colour scarf rings, stacked 8–12 high with one block of sway,
two loose cats at the base; the tallest structure in its region, on the highest
ridge. Lore: the ferry the moon spirits walk down; climbing it is allowed,
finishing it is not — kept one cat short on purpose.

## 4. Ambient life

**Comet-creatures — хвостатики.** Boids of 5–9: a pale head cube with an open
accent mouth-face and two trailing hatch-tail quads. Standard boids plus one
law — a constant bias along the star-wind diagonal, so every flock reads as
swimming upstream through the hatching. A squall scatters them; they regroup
behind it. **Moon spirits — лунники.** Not a flock but a procession: accentLit
heads with ear half-blocks, follow-the-leader on a seeded arc from the sky's
crescent down to the nearest cat-ladder and across the island, bobbing on
offset sine phases; they never break step, even falling. **Owl-leaves —
совы-листья.** Perched instanced eye-block pairs on crests and trumpet rims; a
squall's edge lifts the roost at once — one slow circle, then they resettle.

## 5. Activities

1. **The census.** The collection loop: everything inventoried above is seeded
   somewhere findable — the lion-maned spirit, the grounded snail-shell comet,
   the netted ball, the fallen trumpet, the caterpillars. Standing close marks
   each in a doodle-index whose checklist *is* the painting; he drew the list.
2. **Crest-drifting.** At gravity 20 a running jump from a RIDGE spire floats
   you to the next. Chain crests without touching a valley; squalls crossing
   your line shorten your arc mid-flight and are the whole difficulty.
3. **Squall-reading.** Outrun a visible band along the spit — or step into
   one on purpose, letting woken weight drop you fast from a spire.
4. **Trumpet-organ routes.** Chain trumpet exhales upward to spires no jump
   clears. During a squall every trumpet fires — the risky fast route.
5. **One cat short.** From the top of a cat-ladder, in clean sleeping air, a
   perfectly timed jump touches the procession arc and carries you three heads
   along it before you drift off. The ladder never reaches; you, briefly, can.

## 7. Three kitsch traps

1. **No lullaby.** Gravity sleeps; the world does not. No nightcap on the moon,
   no sleeping face, no yawns, no twinkle audio. His moon is a busy doodled
   crescent with a queue on it — keep the queue businesslike.
2. **No void.** Space here is bright paper, `#B7B6B5`, combed with hatching —
   never a black skybox, nebula purple, or a "dream tint". Six sampled greys are
   the cosmos; darkness is spent only on anchors.
3. **No cute-ification.** His faces are dot-eyed and matter-of-fact: no big
   glossy eyes, no squeaks, no idle giggles. The cats are working, the procession
   keeps step — the wonder is everyone taking the arrangement seriously.
