# Economics — «Экономика»

Dimension lore. Slug `economics`. A subject world: the study's materials plus
one rationed colour — `#EDE6D2` paper · `#C8B392` plaster · `#8A8578` warm
grey · `#4A4741` dark grey · `#23241F` ink · **`#3A4A6B` ledger indigo**,
which marks only what has been balanced or exchanged. Terrain: CONSTRUCTED
(terraced, 3-block steps). Structures: SCALES, seeded tilt, never level.

## 1. The record

Prize-winner, All-Russian Olympiad in Economics, municipal stage, 2026 —
«Призёр, Всероссийская олимпиада школьников по экономике, муниципальный
этап». The note on the door: **he sat the Year 7 paper while in Year 5** —
«писал олимпиаду за 7 класс, обучаясь в 5 классе». Nothing else is claimed.

## 2. The world's story

This is a country where every trade-off is visible. The ground is not a
landscape but a ledger — terraced in even steps, everything entered one line
at a time, so that to climb is to account for the climb. Across the terraces
stand the weigh-beams, pans never level, because the people here know what
the visitor learns slowly: a balance is not a machine for making two things
equal, it is a machine for showing how unequal they are and settling anyway. The
deep law of the place is **opportunity cost — альтернативная стоимость**:
everything here costs not money but the other thing you could have had, and
the country is built so the other thing stays in view — every gate has two
doors, every granary a twin, every path you take keeps the path you didn't
visible over your shoulder, in ink, unspent. The people are not rich and do
not want to be. They are careful, the way a boy sitting a paper two years
early is careful: choosing well is the whole game, and every choice is written down.

## 3. Signature structures

**Weigh-beams — Коромысла.** The generated scales: a dark-grey `#4A4741`
stem 6–14 blocks tall, an indigo `#3A4A6B` beam of 7–11 blocks across the
top, tilted by seed, a 3-block ink `#23241F` pan under each end. True
concept: **supply meets demand where the beam settles, not where it is
level** — a price is a resting place, not a victory. Lore: the country's
landmarks and its law courts; disputes are weighed, and the tilt is the verdict.

**Two-door gates — Ворота о двух дверях.** A plaster `#C8B392` wall, roughly
7×5, pierced by two doorways side by side on a shared path; the lintel of the
door you pass turns indigo behind you, the other stays ink. True concept:
**the cost of any choice is the best alternative you gave up.** Lore: nobody
here says "I went to the terraces"; they say "I didn't go to the mill."

**Exchange courts — Меновые дворы.** Two small plaster stalls (4×3×3) facing
each other across a two-block gap, cream `#EDE6D2` floors, one shared indigo
threshold block between them; goods cross it in both directions or not at
all. True concept: **a voluntary trade leaves both sides better off — that
is why it happens.** Lore: the only indigo underfoot in the world.

**Twin granaries — Амбары-близнецы.** A pair of cream cribs on one terrace,
joined at the base: one open-fronted and half-filled, one closed and full,
their two heights always summing the same. True concept: **saving is not
refusing to spend; it is choosing when.** Lore: every household keeps both,
and argues every evening about the ratio.

## 4. Ambient life

**Tally-moths — Счётные мотыльки.** Pale `#EDE6D2` instanced quads that
gather on a weigh-beam's pans and try to split themselves evenly — but the
seed makes every flock's count odd, so one moth circles forever between the
pans. The country's gentlest lesson: some things do not divide.

**Carriers — Носильщики.** Paired indigo motes drifting between exchange
courts, always two, always in opposite directions — every trade is two
journeys. A mote travelling alone is going home.

## 5. Activities

1. **The barter chain.** A seeded classic: a court offers a jug for your
   apples, another rope for a jug, a third a lantern for rope — and the
   lantern is what the first trader wanted all along. Three to six links per
   region, walked terrace to terrace. Completing one, you have invented the
   argument for money unprompted: a token would have saved the whole walk.
2. **Settle the beam.** A weigh-beam's tilt names a number; nearby terraces
   hold ink weight-stacks of seeded counts. Set the matching stack on the
   light pan: the beam steps toward level — and always stops one block
   short, because the last unit will not split. You can balance a market;
   you cannot flatten one.
3. **Walk the untaken door.** Return to a two-door gate you have passed and
   take the ink door this time. Both lintels end up indigo, and you end up
   knowing what your first choice cost. No reward except the knowledge,
   which is the reward.
4. **The granary walk.** A twin granary offers grain here, now, from the
   open crib — or a marker to a fuller crib two terraces up, its yield
   greater by exactly the climb. Saving pays interest here, and the interest
   is honest: it is the work.
5. **The relay of hands.** Four workshops in a row, each holding one step of
   a task. Carry a piece through all four in order: fast. Do all four steps
   at one bench: slow. Division of labour, measured in your own footsteps.

## 7. What would ruin it

- **Money-rain.** No falling coins, no treasure chests, no gold anything —
  gold is not even in the palette, and that is not an accident. Money enters
  this world once, as the idea at the end of a barter chain, never as loot.
- **Making it about wealth.** No rich quarter, no riches score, no biggest
  granary. The country measures choices, not piles; a world about getting
  would betray both the subject and the boy.
- **Finance costume.** No tickers, charts going up, bulls, bears, crypto
  glyphs or briefcases. This is Year 7 economics — scarcity, trade, the door
  not taken — in the register of a schoolroom, not a trading floor. Any
  colour beyond the six, or any greed in the set dressing, is a lie.
